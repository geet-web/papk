Diploma Program

Diploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharm)

The Diploma in Pharmacy is a 2-year undergraduate diploma course designed to familiarize students with the basic concepts of pharmaceutical science. It is the minimum qualification required to practice or work as a pharmacist in India in a community or hospital pharmacy setting.

Duration 2 Years
Eligibility 10+2 PCB/M
Apply for D.Pharm

Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm)

The Bachelor of Pharmacy is a 4-year degree program providing comprehensive knowledge of medications, drugs, pharmaceutical engineering, and medicinal chemistry. Graduates have a wide scope in research, manufacturing, quality control, and clinical trials across the pharmaceutical industry.

Duration 4 Years
Eligibility 10+2 PCB/M
Apply for B.Pharm
